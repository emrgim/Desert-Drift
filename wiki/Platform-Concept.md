# Desert Drift Platform — Concept

## One-liner
A PWA that brings all the Liwa festival apps together into one experience for spectators and participants, on site and online.

## Users
- Spectators (on site and virtual).
- Participants and drivers.
- Sponsors and organisers.

## Core features
- **One app for the festival**: brings together LSC, the festival app, the vendor app and Platinumlist flows.
- **Ticket sales** with **Apple Pay**.
- **Registration** for competitions and events.
- **Real-time participation**, in person and virtual.
- **Live race tables & results.**
- **Driver profiles** that drivers can customise.
- **Turn-by-turn coordination** of drivers and runs.
- **Virtual racing** against other locations and events (for example, comparing with records set in Riyadh).
- **Sponsor spaces**, physical and virtual. Each space can be sold as virtual only, real only, or both.

## Open questions
- How to integrate with the official apps (APIs vs partnership).
- Timing/telemetry source for live tables.
- Payment provider beyond Apple Pay.

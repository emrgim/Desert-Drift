# Liwa International Festival 2026/27 — Research

_As of 2026-11-13. Programming for 2027 is still "coming soon" on the official site._

## Key dates & place
- Festival: **11 Dec 2026 – 2 Jan 2027** (23 days), Liwa, Al Dhafra Region, Abu Dhabi.
- Main venue: **Tal Moreeb** dune (Moreeb Dune).
- Drifting race: **25–26 Dec 2026** at Tal Moreeb.
- Distance: about **220 km from Dubai**.
- Organisers: DCT Abu Dhabi with Liwa Sports Club (LSC), under the patronage of H.H. Sheikh Hamdan bin Zayed Al Nahyan.

## Audience numbers (2025/26 edition, from liwa2027.com)
- 700,000+ total footfall; 274,440 Liwa Village visitors (40% share).
- 79% male / 21% female.
- AED 88M PR value, 8.3B PR reach, 931M impressions, 319.4M video views.

## Paid zones & prices
- General admission: **free**.
- Liwa Village: tickets about **20–65 AED** (tiers 20 / 25 / 65, plus 10 AED items), via Platinumlist.
- Nitro Circus: **75 AED**, plus a **50 AED** pre-show.
- VIP areas, competitions, camping: priced in the LSC app (TBD).
- Adventure extras: ziplines, maze, bungee, paintball and similar.

## Nitro Circus
- Action-sports touring show (FMX, BMX, scooters, stunts) founded by **Travis Pastrana**.
- Liwa shows: **19–20 Dec 2026**, 15+ athletes.

## Retail & food
- **22 shops**: 14 in the Souk, 8 in Meydan.
- About **30 food & drink points**.
- Artisans and crafts, Auto zone, Tal Moreeb Market, ADNOC Energy Camp.

## Festival site layout (sponsorable destinations)
1. Liwa Village
2. Tal Moreeb
3. Liwa Walk
4. Moreeb Dune Park
5. Tal Moreeb Market
6. ADNOC Energy Camp

## Six Liwa Village sub-zones (sponsor zones)
1. **Shams Liwa** — Ferris wheel, panoramic views.
2. **Adrenaline** — Maze Runner, ziplines.
3. **Meydan** — skill games, RC cars, horror rooms, kids' 4x4 track.
4. **Souk** — artisans, Emirati craft, dining, retail.
5. **Auto** — classic cars, displays, mini jeep tracks.
6. **Wanasa** — family zone for ages 1–12.

## Eight festival worlds
1. Motorsport & Off-Road
2. Emirati Culture

4. Retail & F&B
5. Adventure Attractions
6. Night Shows
7. Camping & Hospitality
8. Tourism

## Apps & registration
- **Liwa Sports Club (LSC) app** (iOS/Android): account, competition registration (car uphill drag, dune bashing, motorbike drag, falconry), camping, pricing.
- **Official festival app** (new): calendar, announcements, camping and RV bookings, commercial participation.
- **Vendor app**: Souk vendors pick a plot, upload documents, pay after approval, get a QR pass.
- **Platinumlist**: Liwa Village and Nitro Circus tickets.
- Volunteers: **volunteers.ae**.
- Newsletter: form on liwainternationalfestival.ae/en/events; there is no visitor account on the site.
